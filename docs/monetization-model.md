# HomeSphere Monetization Model

## Revenue Streams
- Service commission (dynamic/fixed/category/vendor-specific)
- Franchise revenue share
- Subscription AMC plans (monthly/quarterly/yearly)
- Lead fee for premium interior projects
- Featured vendor placements in search
- Convenience fee for instant/emergency bookings

## Unit Economics Formula
- **Gross Booking Value (GBV)** = service subtotal + add-ons
- **Net Platform Revenue** = commissions + convenience fee + subscription amortization - referral payouts - payment gateway fees - discounts

## Referral Economics
- Configurable by role and depth (`L1`, optional `L2/L3`).
- Cap monthly payouts by wallet and KYC level.
- Fraud checks: device fingerprint, velocity limits, duplicate household detection.

## Retention Levers
- Wallet cashback on repeat booking
- Tiered loyalty (silver/gold/platinum)
- AMC preventive maintenance nudges
