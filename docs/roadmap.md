# HomeSphere Full Project Roadmap

## Phase 0 (Weeks 1-2): Discovery & Foundations
- Domain modeling and final BRD
- UX research, persona mapping
- Engineering standards, CI/CD bootstrap, infra baseline

## Phase 1 (Weeks 3-8): MVP Launch
- OTP auth + profiles + addresses
- Customer booking core + live tracking
- Vendor app job lifecycle + proofs
- Admin panel user/vendor/booking management
- Payment rails (Razorpay + COD)
- Wallet + basic referral rewards

## Phase 2 (Weeks 9-14): Growth Engine
- Franchise panel + city management
- Dynamic commission engine
- Multi-level referral option
- Subscription plans (AMC)
- CRM ticketing + notifications
- Cashfree integration + refunds

## Phase 3 (Weeks 15-20): Interior Suite
- Designer workflows (2D/3D uploads, moodboards)
- BOQ generation and material modules
- Client approval workflows
- Milestone and timeline tracking

## Phase 4 (Weeks 21-26): Intelligence & Scale
- AI room budget estimation
- AI service recommendation engine
- Voice booking support
- Advanced analytics and anomaly detection
- Performance hardening + DR drills

## Phase 5 (Ongoing): Optimization
- A/B testing funnels
- Fraud/risk refinement
- Cost optimization + autoscaling policy tuning
